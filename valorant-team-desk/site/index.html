Before you install: VANTA Team Desk connects to Supabase for account sign-in and team collaboration, which require an internet connection. Scrim notes and the practice tracker are local; the app does not read VALORANT game data or collect player combat stats. The Windows installer is currently unsigned, so Windows may show a SmartScreen warning.
