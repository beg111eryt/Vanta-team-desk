VANTA Team Desk is an independent team planning tool. It does not connect to VALORANT or access game data.

Write down the setup, the cue, and the job each player owns.

A simple reflection on the matches you log. These are team notes, not a judgement.

Focus win rates are descriptive only: a small log cannot establish that a focus area caused a result.
